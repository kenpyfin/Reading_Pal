## Objectives

1. Replace the New reading guide modal with a toolbar angle capsule and `PromptConfigPopover` (Radix Themes `Popover` + `SegmentedControl`).
2. Surface quick angle presets, a custom lens, and depth/emphasis controls.
3. Persist `prompt_depth` and `prompt_emphasis` on the guide and pass them into roadmap generation.
4. Keep the current high-granularity segmentation when depth is omitted or `standard`. Compact is the only mode that asks for 4–6 pillars. `high_fidelity` reinforces a detailed list and does not cap pillars at 8–12 (that cap would fight the knowledge-mapping substance gates).

## API

`POST /api/books/{book_id}/reading-guides` body:

- `name` optional
- `custom_requirements` optional
- `prompt_depth`: `compact` | `standard` | `high_fidelity`
- `prompt_emphasis`: `balanced` | `author_craft` | `takeaways`

Regenerate reuses the stored fields.
